Designed for the Realities of Student Freelancing

Large freelancing platforms feel intimidating, require extensive 50-job reviews, and ignore small campus tasks. SkillBridge strips away the noise.

🎓 Persona 1: Aisha (Student Freelancer)

"I have skills, but no 5-year portfolio."

Second-year student good at design and video editing. Intimidated by Fiverr algorithms and client bidding wars. SkillBridge gives her small, approachable campus tasks to build her very first paid track record.

  • 1 Sign up with DOB + Student ID proof (18+ verified)
  • 2 Pick offered skills (Design, Editing, Code, PPT)
  • 3 Apply directly with rate bid and turnaround time
🙋 Persona 2: Rohan (Campus Club Lead)

"I need a poster by Friday on a ₹500 budget."

Organizes college events and urgently needs quick deliverables without agency overhead. He needs someone affordable who won't disappear and whose identity is verified.

  • 1 Select the skill required for the upcoming event
  • 2 Browse verified student creators in the talent directory
  • 3 Send task details, budget & deadline directly

Campus Trust & Safety Architecture

Keeping campus freelance interactions safe, accountable, and transparent.

⚠️ PRD Section 8: Prototype Limitations & Production Roadmap

As defined in Section 8 of the Product Requirements Document, the current prototype verifies that a valid document file was provided and confirms the entered Date of Birth meets the 18+ requirement.

In Prototype Scope:
  • 18+ Date of birth gating check
  • Compulsory ID file attachment for providers
  • Simulated anti-tampering scan & badge issuance
  • In-browser memory session management
Required for Live Production:
  • DigiLocker / Aadhaar eKYC integration (India)
  • Automated OCR to extract DoB and photo
  • Liveness / Selfie facial match heuristics
  • Encrypted government database cross-checks
